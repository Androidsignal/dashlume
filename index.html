Product overview

DashLume turns an ordinary video call into a record that works for you. Start a meeting in one tap or join with a six-character code, and the app handles the rest: media flows through a self-hosted WebRTC SFU, speech is transcribed live, and an AI participant sits in the chat ready to answer questions about what was just said.

The moment you hang up, the transcript is condensed into a summary and a list of action items, then stored in your meeting history — searchable weeks later. It is built as a single Flutter codebase for Android and iOS, backed by Firebase for auth, data and notifications, so the whole experience stays fast on real mobile networks rather than only on office Wi-Fi.

What broke, and what fixed it

Real bugs from real devices, and the change that actually solved each one.

Second participant joined and saw a black tile.

Renegotiated the peer connection on every new producer instead of only on the first join.

Calls died silently on mobile data handoff.

Added ICE connection-state monitoring with automatic restart and a visible reconnecting state.

Transcripts arrived out of order and duplicated lines.

Keyed each utterance by speaker + start timestamp and merged interim results before commit.

Rooms stayed alive forever after everyone left.

A periodic sweep closes abandoned rooms and releases SFU transports.

AI replies blocked the chat while generating.

Streamed the response into a placeholder message so the call and chat stay interactive.

Echo when two phones sat in the same room.

Enabled acoustic echo cancellation and auto-gain on the native audio track constraints.
